## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

First public release.

### Added

- Menu bar app with a global shortcut (`⌃⌥N` by default) that opens a floating panel over any app, including full-screen apps, without switching Spaces.
- Two writing styles: **Simple** (like Notes: tick circles, bullets and titles with a formatting toolbar) and **Markdown** (live styling with visible syntax).
- Checklists you tick with a click, lists that continue on `↩`, numbered lists, headings, bold, italic, code, quotes and clickable links.
- Natural-language reminders (`@tomorrow 2pm`, `@demain 14h`, `@in 30min`…) delivered as macOS notifications.
- `#tags` with one-click filtering, favorites, archive, and a Trash kept for 30 days with undo.
- Search across every note, keyboard navigation and shortcuts for everything.
- Keep-on-top mode, hideable note list, light and dark themes, font family and size.
- Settings for the shortcut, language (English or French), reminders, launch at login and Markdown export.
- Local storage in a single JSON file with a backup at every launch; nothing leaves your Mac.
